DictaMux

Voice-control your fleet of agents.

You are running five coding agents across as many tmux panes. DictaMux lets you steer all of them by voice — name the pane you mean, speak the instruction, edit it mid-sentence, approve the one that is waiting on you — without stopping to hunt for the window or taking your eyes off the one you are actually reading.

What you need

  • MacApple Silicon only — M1 or later. Intel Macs cannot run the on-device speech models.
  • macOSmacOS 26 (Tahoe) or later.
  • Terminaltmux. DictaMux drives the sessions you already have; it does not replace them.
  • PermissionsMicrophone, Speech Recognition, and Accessibility — three separate macOS prompts on first run. The quick-start walks through all three.
